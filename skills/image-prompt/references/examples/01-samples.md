# 完整示例

用户说：「画个雪莉在雨夜石阶上捡铜钱」

雪莉在人物库里，外观取 `face` + `bodySFW` + `lowerSFW`。

最终串：

`A medium close-up of the girl crouching on wet stone steps, setting down a paper lantern with one hand while reaching toward a coin half-buried in the mud, rain dripping from the edge of her bamboo hat, bamboo grove looming behind her in the dark, white hair, one pale gold eye, small frame, wet grey short robe, upper body, crouching, reaching out, bamboo hat, wet clothes, night, rain, backlighting, from side`

拆开看：

- 主干：镜头（medium close-up）→ 场景（wet stone steps）→ 角色（the girl crouching）→ 动作（setting down a lantern, reaching toward a coin）
- 句尾：外观 + 衣服 + 景别 + 姿势 + 天气 + 时间 + 打光
- 镜头写了 `from side`，因为正面拍不到伸手够东西的侧影
